# AIR COMMAND demo video structure

**Target runtime:** 2:45–3:00. The video should show the working browser prototype and explain what is implemented. Use the shot list and narration in `docs/demo/` as the recording source.

## Story

1. Establish the problem statement and prototype in the first 15 seconds.
2. Show the console with its seeded exercise data and map.
3. Demonstrate COA generation and explain the actual weighted rules and constraints.
4. Show weapon compatibility, the rule-based RED VIEW, briefing review and local simulation.
5. Close on AAR, limitations and a specific future validation path.

## Evidence and claim boundaries

The checked-in screenshots are 1920×1080 captures of the prototype. Show real app states or these screenshots. Keep exercise and simulated labels visible where relevant. Success/risk values are illustrative. Do not claim live IACCS or sensor integration, operational ATO generation, AI/ML optimization, measured decision-time improvement, field validation or deployment readiness.
